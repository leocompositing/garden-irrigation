New Turf Establishment Programme

Hydrawise HC Pro · Zones 1 & 3 · Late May / early June 2026 · 28-day window

Context

Rolled turf going down across both lawn zones. New turf needs a totally different programme to mature lawn — frequent shallow watering for the first 2 weeks, tapering over 4 weeks before switching to normal Smart Watering. This document is the schedule to enter into the Hydrawise app.

Site orientation

Per the IRRISketch compass (144° SE) and the sun chart: view direction from the back of the house is 144° SE. House sits at the NW end of the property; garden extends SE.

  • Zone 3 (bottom lawn) is the lower tier nearest the house, NW end.
  • Zone 1 (top lawn) is the upper tier furthest from house, SE end, beyond the retaining wall. 16.2 m long × ~7.2 m wide.
  • Trees at the SE far end shade the last 6 m of Zone 1 (~43 m² of 116 m², ~37% of area). Heads in that strip are consumers 11, 13, 14 in the IRRISketch sprinkler table (MP1000-90s and the MP2000-90 at the top of the map).

Zone 1's open middle is the limiting area (highest ET). The tree-shaded SE strip is the over-watering risk — same valve, same run time, half the evaporation. Watch it closely (fungal disease, moss, slow drainage). Long-term you may want to dial back the radius on heads 11/13/14.

Watering rules during establishment

Do NOT enable Smart Watering / Predictive during the 28-day establishment. ET-based scheduling assumes established roots and will under-water turf. Use Time-Based with rain sensor only.
  • Day 0 (laying day) — manual soak: immediately after the last roll, run Zone 1: 3 × 20 min with 30 min between (~22 mm), Zone 3: 3 × 15 min with 30 min between (~26 mm). Walk it — soil under the rolls should be wet 50 mm down.
  • No foot traffic for the full 14 days.
  • First mow only when grass reaches ~65 mm and roots resist a gentle tug.
  • Rain sensor: keep enabled — skip a cycle only if >5 mm rain fell in the last 12 h. Don't trust the forecast.

Verification checklist

  1. Day 0: Lift a corner of turf at 3 random points per zone — soil under the roll should be wet 50 mm down. If dry, run another 10 min on that zone.
  2. Day 3: Walk both lawns mid-morning. Turf should feel cool and damp, never crispy or curling at edges. Adjust Phase 1 sessions ±5 min if needed.
  3. Day 7: Check for fungal disease (slimy patches, fairy rings, mushrooms) — over-watering risk. Pay special attention to the tree-shaded SE 6 m of Zone 1 and the house-side strip of Zone 3, which dry far slower. If you see disease in the Zone 1 shaded strip, remediate in this order: (a) physically dial down the radius/arc on heads 11, 13, 14; (b) tape over part of those nozzles; (c) shorten Zone 1's overall sessions by 5 min.
  4. Day 14: Tug test (above) — decides Phase 3 entry.
  5. Day 21: First mow check — grass at 60–70 mm, mow to 45 mm max.
  6. Day 28: Final walk for dry/yellow patches. If any, nudge MP Rotator radius screws rather than extending the programme. Then flip to Smart Watering.
  7. Week 6 (Day 42): Check Hydrawise Reports — Smart Watering should be applying ~25 mm/week to Zone 1, ~20 mm/week to Zone 3.
