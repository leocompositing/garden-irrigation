New Turf Establishment Programme

Hydrawise HC Pro · Zones 1 & 3 · Late May / early June 2026 · 28-day window

Context

Rolled turf going down across both lawn zones. New turf needs a totally different programme to mature lawn — frequent shallow watering for the first 2 weeks, tapering over 4 weeks before switching to normal Smart Watering. This document is the schedule to enter into the Hydrawise app.

Watering rules during establishment

Do NOT enable Smart Watering / Predictive during the 28-day establishment. ET-based scheduling assumes established roots and will under-water turf. Use Time-Based mode, with the rainfall weather trigger as the only automatic skip (see Rain handling below).
  • Day 0 (laying day) — manual soak: immediately after the last roll, run Zone 1: 2 × 45 min with 30 min between (~11 mm), Zone 3: 2 × 15 min with 30 min between (~10 mm). Then lift corners at 3 points per zone — soil under the rolls must be wet 50 mm down. If not, run one more cycle on that zone (Zone 1: 45 min, Zone 3: 15 min). Zone 1's low 7.4 mm/h rate means long runs are safe; Zone 3's ~21 mm/h is not — never exceed 15 min per cycle there.
  • No foot traffic for the full 14 days.
  • First mow only when grass reaches ~65 mm and roots resist a gentle tug.
  • Rain handling (no physical rain sensor installed): use Hydrawise's weather triggers, set exactly as follows during establishment:
    • "Don't water when forecast below 20°C" — OFF ⚠️ (a normal Surrey June day is 18–21°C; ON would skip half of all days)
    • "Don't water when 80%+ chance of rain" — OFF (forecast-based; doesn't measure what actually fell)
    • "Don't water when 3mm+ rainfall last day" — ON ✓ (raise threshold to 5 mm if editable; if turf looks dry after a marginal skip, Run Now manually)
    • "Don't water when 38.1mm+ rainfall last 3 days" — ON ✓ (deluge backstop)
    • "Adjust watering — water longer when hot" — OFF ⚠️ (auto-extended runs break Zone 3's 15-min cycle cap)
    • "Adjust watering — water more often when hot" — OFF (in a heatwave, add the 12:30 cycle manually instead)
    For a prolonged soggy spell that never trips the trigger, walk the lawn — if soil under the rolls is squelchy, hit Suspend for 24 h. Skip because the ground is wet, never because rain is forecast. Revisit the temperature toggles after the Day-29 switch to Smart Watering.
  • Set up all four phases now using Grow In Program (program → Advanced Settings → Grow In Program): create the four programs ("P1 Saturation", "P2 Root chase", "P3 Taper", "P4 Transition"), toggle off "Start immediately" / "Stop never" on each, and set date windows counting from lay day — P1: lay day → +7, P2: +8 → +14, P3: +15 → +21, P4: +22 → +28. Each program expires as the next starts, no app visits needed at boundaries. Phase 1 needs two Program Start Times (05:30 + 17:30) with both zones attached. Two things stay manual: if the day-14 tug test fails, push P2 stop / P3 start back 3–4 days; and the Day-29 switch to Smart Watering is a mode change you make by hand. Leave Monthly Adjustments at 100% for every month — establishment run times must not be scaled.

Programme — Time-Based schedule on HC Pro

One programme per phase, both zones attached — four programmes total. Zones never water simultaneously: Hydrawise runs the zones in a programme sequentially, so Zone 3 starts automatically the moment Zone 1 finishes (and during Zone 1's soak pauses in Phases 3–4, the controller slots Zone 3's cycles into the gaps). Phase 1 is one programme with two start times (05:30 + 17:30). Sequential running is also hydraulically correct — one valve at a time keeps full pressure at the heads.

Verification checklist

  1. Day 0: Lift a corner of turf at 3 random points per zone — soil under the roll should be wet 50 mm down. If dry, run another cycle on that zone (Zone 1: 45 min, Zone 3: 15 min).
  2. Day 3: Walk both lawns mid-morning. Turf should feel cool and damp, never crispy or curling at edges. Adjust Phase 1 sessions ±5 min if needed.
  3. Day 7: Check for fungal disease (slimy patches, fairy rings, mushrooms) — over-watering risk. Pay special attention to the tree-shaded SE 6 m of Zone 1 and the house-side strip of Zone 3, which dry far slower. If you see disease in the Zone 1 shaded strip, remediate in this order: (a) physically dial down the radius/arc on heads #1, #6, #7 (the two MP1000-90s + the 4.7 m MP2000-90 by the tree corner); (b) swap their PRS40 cartridges for PRS30 (2.1 bar — cuts flow ~10–15%, no digging); (c) shorten Zone 1's overall sessions by 5 min.
  4. Day 14: Tug test (above) — decides Phase 3 entry.
  5. Day 21: First mow check — grass at 60–70 mm, mow to 45 mm max.
  6. Day 28: Final walk for dry/yellow patches. If any, nudge MP Rotator radius screws rather than extending the programme. Then flip to Smart Watering.
  7. Week 6 (Day 42): Check Hydrawise Reports — Smart Watering should be applying ~25 mm/week to Zone 1, ~20 mm/week to Zone 3.
